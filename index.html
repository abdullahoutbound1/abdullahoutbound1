A little about me

Practical outbound support for growing businesses.

I handle the full outbound process, from finding the right prospects and building verified lead lists to personalized outreach, follow-ups, lead qualification, and appointment setting. My focus is simple: turn cold prospects into qualified conversations and sales calls.

For B2B Lead Generation, I research companies and decision-makers based on your ICP, industry, location, company size, job title, and other targeting criteria. For LinkedIn Outreach, I handle prospect research, connection requests, messaging, follow-ups, and campaign tracking. For Cold Email, I handle prospect sourcing, email verification, campaign setup, sequences, follow-ups, and campaign monitoring using Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo, and Sales Navigator.
